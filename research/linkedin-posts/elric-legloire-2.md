There is no single best B2B data provider. Stop shopping for one.  
  
I tested 10 on the same 1,400 US contacts, checked every number twice (mobile, then right person).   
  
The winner flips completely depending on what you measure:  
- Widest coverage? Wiza. A valid mobile for 86% of a list. Mid-pack on accuracy.  
- Most accurate? RocketReach, the right person 69% of the time. Reaches far fewer people.  
- Cheapest per usable number? LeadMagic at $0.11. ZoomInfo is the priciest at $1.27, more than 10x.  
  
Plot all 10 on coverage versus accuracy and the top-right corner, the wide-AND-accurate one, is empty. No tool lives there. Reach wide and you give up accuracy. Nail accuracy and you give up reach.  
  
And the gap is expensive: across every valid mobile, only 60% were the right person. Even the most accurate tool hands you a stranger 1 in 4 times.  
  
So "which provider is best" was never the right question.   
The real one: for YOUR context, which tool reaches wide, and which one confirms the person? You don't just pick one. But you stack what works best your business.  
  
My take, not the data's: stop buying data on reputation.   
The favorite-provider answer is an opinion.
