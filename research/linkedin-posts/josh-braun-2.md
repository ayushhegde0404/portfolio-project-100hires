You ask a question.  
  
The prospect pauses.  
  
It feels awkward.  
  
So you jump in.  
You clarify.  
You explain.  
You answer the question you just asked.  
  
And just like that, you stole the moment.  
  
The silence wasn’t the problem.  
  
Your discomfort with it was.  
  
The way out?  
  
After asking a question or making an observation:  
  
Zip the lip.  
  
Count to five.  
  
Let the silence do the work.  
  
People can’t think and listen at the same time.  
  
The seller who talks first learns less.  
  
The seller who waits hears what’s really going on.
