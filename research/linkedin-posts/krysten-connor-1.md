This is the thing we don’t talk about enough with top performers. We're not worried about missing quota. We're worried we're one-hit wonders. That we got lucky. That we can't repeat it in the next role, or even the next quarter:  
  
"I've been in the top three every year, but I've never been number one."  
  
A client said this to me last week, so I asked her what she thought was holding her back.  
  
She said, "I don't have a system. So I feel like everything has to be customized. And it takes SO much time that it limits how many deals I can work."  
  
And here's why the "one hit wonder" fear makes sense: there's this belief that every deal is a special snowflake. That what worked last time won't work this time. That we just have to figure it out fresh every single time.  
  
The problem is we were never taught otherwise.  
  
Companies teach us how to adhere to their selling process. They teach us nothing about aligning with a buying process. What we put in Salesforce is essentially so our leaders can forecast deals. It does not help us progress them.  
  
So we hit quota, but we have no repeatable process. We close the big one, but we're not confident we could do it again. We look successful on the outside, while quietly terrified on the inside. (I've BEEN this person.)  
  
This is the unlock: Having a repeatable system. Not a CRM stage. Not a forecast category. A framework we can follow no matter what deal we're in. A scaffolding.  
  
Because the difference between top 3 and number 1 is knowing exactly what we did that worked, so we can do it again on purpose.
