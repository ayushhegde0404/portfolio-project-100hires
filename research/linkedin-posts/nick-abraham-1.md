>50% of outbound success is following up (tastefully) until you make the sale or get told to F off. This is a great example:  
  
1. Person cold calls Daniel: he doesn't answer.  
2. Tries a couple weeks later: he doesn't answer.  
3. Tries 11 days later: They talk for 27 mins.  
  
Most sellers would have stopped after not getting a pick-up from call 1.   
  
The same goes for cold email.   
  
Outside of leads that explicitly say you're not interested, you should reach them again 30 days post-initial touch.   
  
Just because they aren't ready right now does not mean they won’t be eventually.
