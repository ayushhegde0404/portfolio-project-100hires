“What makes you different from {competitor}?”  
  
IT’S A TRAP! (Admiral Ackbar)  
  
Most salespeople immediately jump into their battlecards:  
  
We offer more integrations, our pricing is fairer, our offering is easier to use, we offer whitelabelling to improve brand, they don’t have SSO, a regular towel doesn’t work wet but this works wet or dry (Shamwow Guy)   
  
NO! No no no no no no no (Freaky Nikki)  
  
Never open your mouth before you know what the shot is (Al Capone)   
  
Instead you might say:  
  
“{competitor}? We differ in a few ways. I guess what’s making them top of mind?”  
  
“We used them in the past and had a bad experience”  
  
“Oh sorry to hear that, what specific areas were an issue?”   
  
“Well they only integrated with half of our partners, which meant we often had to either duct tape an integration or just do the work manually”   
  
“Oh okay, got it. I’ve heard they have great integrations with Google Analytics, but other systems have definately been a sticking point for marketing teams. What connections were causing the most issues?”  
  
“Meta & LinkedIn were the main ones”  
  
“And what were you looking to actually do with Meta & LinkedIn”   
  
“Push our content & ads to them and receive analytics back in one place. But because we had to do that manually for two of our main channels, it defeated the point of even having a tool”  
  
Now you know the shot you can:  
  
A) Challenge their position if you don’t have those integrations “You mentioned content, most teams do {this}, what stopped you from taking that approach?”   
  
B) Share your differentiators if you do have said integrations “Yeah those platforms are vital for most advertising teams, which is why we have native connections that allow content and data to flow directly so you don’t have to check more than one place for answers”
